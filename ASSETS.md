# Landing object studies

The five transparent cutouts in `public/objects` were created for Pezzi with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on 26 September 2026. They are original generated examples rather than client uploads or assets from the design references.

| Asset | Generation direction | Landing use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `poppy.png` | A single coral-orange poppy with a green stem, isolated as a transparent cutout | Flower mosaic loop |
| `sneaker.png` | An unbranded cobalt and lavender sneaker, isolated as a transparent cutout | Hero mosaic loop |
| `lemon.png` | A lemon with a cross-section slice and leaves, isolated as a transparent cutout | Citrus mosaic loop |
| `camera.png` | An unbranded turquoise instant camera with coral details, isolated on transparent alpha | Floating hero study |
| `teapot.png` | A sculptural cherry-red ceramic teapot, isolated on transparent alpha | Floating hero study |

The WebM loops and PNG posters in `public/demos` were rendered locally with Pezzi's `drawMosaic` engine from those cutouts. Each `-hd` video is 1920×1080 with a 112-column mosaic, source colors, and a high-bitrate VP9 encode; it loops the subject's movement twice. The posters have the same 1920×1080 resolution and show the first rendered frame. No external footage or reference-site artwork is included. The posters provide a visual fallback while video loads and for reduced-motion visitors.
